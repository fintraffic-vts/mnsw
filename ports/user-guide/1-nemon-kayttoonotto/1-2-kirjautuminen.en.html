1.2 Login

Log in to NEMO via the Suomi.fi identification service. The service enables secure login to electronic services and verifies the user's identity.

For detailed instructions on Suomi.fi identification, see: Suomi.fi – How to log in to an e-service that uses Suomi.fi identification

This is how you log in

  • open the browser and go to the address mnsw.mnsw.fi

  • press Log in

  • Select an identification method in Suomi.fi view

After logging in, you will see the NEMO home page. You can access the views and functions permitted by your user role from the main menu.
