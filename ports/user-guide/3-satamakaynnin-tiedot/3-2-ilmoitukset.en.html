Review notifications related to port operations Notification view. View opens when you select your port or port area Port call Port calls view.

Notification header and answer information (RES + FRM)

Each notification shall relate to its identification, transmission and the title and answer information required for processing.

MAI contains the title of the notification to be sent. After sending the notification, it shall consist of: RES + FRM section containing notification processing .

Information:

  • the notification is linked to the correct port operation; and to the notifier

  • initial notification and subsequent updates; identified

  • the notice is transmitted to the hearing in the correct form;

  • the response to the hearing is linked to the right notification

  • Show whether the declaration is accepted or required measures.

MAI, RES + FRM sections appear as part of the notification form the specific view.

MAI - Title of declaration

The MAI section contains a declaration for identification, transmission and information necessary for monitoring. These include:

  • identification of the declaration and Port call;

  • type of declaration

  • information on the notifier.

NEMO automatically forms a Port call for the MAI section, on the basis of the declaration and the organisational information of the user. MAI section the information cannot be changed by means of a notification form.

RES - Title of reply

The RES section includes a response to identification and original the information necessary to link the declaration. These include: For example:

  • response identifier

  • response type

  • authority that issued the response

  • a reference to the declaration to which the reply relates.

The information in the RESS section cannot be changed.

FRM - Answer notification

The FRM section contains the outcome of the notification process. It shows:

  • state of the handling of the declaration;

  • any additional information;

  • virheet

  • varoitukset.

Error information may also indicate the field or row of tables of the declaration, to which the feedback relates.

The data for RES and FRM sections cannot be changed.

Delegated verification of the response to the notification

Once you have sent a notification, check it a response from the reporting perspective.

On the basis of the answer, the following action:

  • If the declaration is:approved, answer no changes need to be made.

  • If the declaration is:was given conditional approval, check the additional information and any follow-up to the reply.

  • If the declaration is:rejected or incomplete, check the reported errors, correct the notification and send it Again.

  • Also check the warnings and assess whether they require data repair.

The accepted FRM reply confirms that the notification has been accepted to be discussed. This does not, as such, mean a decision on the ship's liquidation, a permit of departure or any other separate decision of the authority.
