WAR - Waste delivery receipt on arrival

WAR Waste delivery receipt on arrival

The WAR notification provides actual information about the vessel in port waste delivered to reception facilities. WAR corresponds on the disposal of waste under Directive (EU) 2019 / 883 on ship waste the supporting document to be provided.

As a user of the port organisation, you can submit WAR notification on the ship if the notification is delegated to your organisation. Complete the notification by the supplier of port reception facilities on the basis of a supporting document and ensure that the information provided is equivalent to: actual delivery.

Submit WAR notification before departure or as soon as waste the proof of delivery has been obtained.

Read more about the reception of waste from ships: Traficom - Leaving waste in port

Please provide an WAR notification for each waste delivery. Usually WAR is given in addition to the WAS pre-notification. Where on board: is an exemption from the WAS notification obligation granted by Traficom, There is no need to issue a WAS notification. The actual delivery of waste shall: However, WAR should be notified.

WAR notification obligation

An exemption from the obligation to deliver waste in port or from the WAS notification obligation does not remove the obligation to submit a WAR notification. When waste has actually been delivered to port, a WAR notification must be submitted.

The WAR declaration shall be submitted for each waste implemented supply, i.e. whenever waste has been delivered to port. Ship exemption from the waste disposal obligation and the WAS notification obligation; exempt WAR from reporting.

One or several WAR notifications during the same Port call

Enter first one WAR declaration when the waste is delivered at the same time times, on the same platform and on the same port reception equipment the supplier.

Add another or more WAR notifications if Port call during:

  • the waste was delivered at different platforms or terminals;

  • the waste has been delivered to different reception facilities; suppliers

  • deliveries take place at different times (e.g. upon arrival); and before departure)

Each WAR notification describes one transaction in your own location, recipient and time period.
